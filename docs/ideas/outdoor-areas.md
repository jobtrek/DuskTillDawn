# Idea: outdoor areas

Parked for M2. Needs an outdoor map, new mini-games and new art. See [game-v1.md](../game-v1.md#11-parked-ideas-not-in-v1).

## 1. Outdoor Areas Around the Building

### Generator Yard
- Backup diesel generator used when both solar panels and the power grid fail.
- Requires regular refueling.
- Can fail to start on particularly cold nights, requiring maintenance or repairs.

### Mini Substation
- Allows the player to manually switch between solar, grid, and generator power.
- Used during power outages, lightning strikes, or electrical failures.
- Requires the player to physically go outside and operate the switches.

### Fiber Cable Trench
- Contains the building's internet connection.
- Construction crews may accidentally cut the fiber cable, causing an internet outage.
- The player can:
  - Go outside and repair/splice the cable.
  - Switch to a backup internet provider.

### Delivery Dock
- Where replacement hardware, supplies, drivers, and service personnel arrive.
- Potential location for physical supply-chain attacks.
- The player must inspect deliveries and decide whether to allow them inside.

### Front Gate & Badge Reader
- Main entrance to the facility.
- Visitors and delivery personnel request access.
- The player must decide whether to grant or deny entry.

### Parking Lot
- Area for employee and visitor vehicles.
- Can serve as an additional location for security-related incidents.

### Cooling Units
- AC condensers located on the roof or behind the building.
- Responsible for keeping the servers at a safe operating temperature.
- During heat waves, cooling systems may struggle or fail.
- The player may need to inspect, repair, or temporarily cool the units using a hose.


## 3. Outdoor Exploration & Gameplay Mechanics

### Limited Visibility at Night
- Outdoor areas become dark at night.
- The player's visibility is limited.
- A flashlight is required to safely navigate and inspect equipment.

### Time & Distance Management
- Moving between outdoor locations takes time.
- While the player is outside, incidents and alerts can continue piling up inside the building.
- This creates a trade-off between fixing outdoor problems and managing internal emergencies.

**Possible upgrades to reduce time costs:**
- **Tablet:** Monitor the facility dashboard while outside.
- **Golf Cart:** Travel between outdoor locations faster.
- **Drone:** Inspect solar panels, rooftops, and other hard-to-reach areas remotely.
- **Security Cameras:** Monitor outdoor areas without physically visiting them.
