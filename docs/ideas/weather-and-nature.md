# Idea: weather and nature events

Parked for M2. Needs the outdoor world.


### Snow
- Snow accumulates on the solar panels, reducing their efficiency.
- The player must go outside and manually sweep the panels clean.

### Storms
- Strong winds can knock the radio dish out of alignment.
- The player must go outside and realign it.
- **Mini-game:** Rotate the dish until the signal strength bar reaches its maximum.

### Floods
- Heavy rain can cause flooding in the basement.
- The player must react quickly by:
  - Placing sandbags.
  - Activating water pumps.
  - Repairing or inspecting drainage systems.

### Lightning
- Lightning strikes can damage electrical equipment.
- May cause power outages or force the player to manually switch power sources at the substation.

### Animals
- Animals can cause unexpected infrastructure problems.
- Examples:
  - Squirrels chewing through outdoor cables.
  - Rats entering the server room.
  - Birds interfering with rooftop equipment.
