# Idea: daytime maintenance rounds

Parked for M3. Doubles the content; v1 uses only the shop between nights.

- During the day, the player can walk around the facility and perform preventive maintenance.
- These rounds help identify problems before they become serious.
- Proper maintenance reduces the likelihood of incidents occurring at night.

**Examples of preventive maintenance:**
- Checking generator fuel levels.
- Inspecting cooling units.
- Checking cables for damage.
- Cleaning solar panels.
- Inspecting gates and access systems.
- Checking drainage and water pumps.

Good daytime maintenance makes nighttime survival easier, while neglecting the outdoor infrastructure increases the likelihood of emergencies.
