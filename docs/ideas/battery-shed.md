# Idea: battery shed

Parked for M2. **Unfinished:** the source text stops mid-sentence at the thermal runaway incident.

 **What's there**
  - Rows of battery racks with little lights on each module: green,yellow or red.
  - Battery management system(BMS) panel showing charge, temperature and health of each rack 
  - Inverter that turns battery power into usable power for the servers, with a humming fan 
  - Ventilation fans and AC unit on the wall, since batteries hate heat.
  - Fire suppression system with pipes along the ceiling, a red alarm light and an emergency shutoff button by the door
  - Cable trench running to the main building and the generator yard
  - Spare module shelf for swapping out bad batteries

 **The core mechanic**
   - Daytime charging. How full the batteries get depends on the weather and on the solar panels on the roof being clean and working.
   - Night drain. Every running server uses power. The dashboard shows how many hours you have left at the current load.
   - Choices. If the batteries won't last until sunrise, you can shut down non-critical servers, switching to the grid, or start the generator and burn fuel.

 **Battery mini-game**
  - Swap a bad module. Find the red module on the rack, power it down safely in the right order,pull it and slide in a spare. Skipping the power-down gives you a shock and a penalty.
  - Balance the racks. Some racks drain faster than others. Move load between them to keep them even, like a sliding puzzle.
  - Cooling control. Adjust fans and AC. More cooling keeps batteires healthy but uses power itself.
